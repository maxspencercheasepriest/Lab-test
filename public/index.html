      How to play

      Ring the four chapel bells. Find the three film reels (they move every Lesson) and return them to the projector. Someone stands on the plate in the old classroom to raise the gate while someone else fetches the study key. Then unlock the exit and go home.

      The staff patrol the wing. If they glimpse you they'll come and look; if they get a proper look they'll chase. Walk quietly, crouch past them, mind the broken glass. Hide in lockers or under beds. Throw bottles and bricks to distract them — or hit them to stun. Slam doors in their faces.

      The Orderlies beat you down; a friend can pick you up. The Prefect grabs you — mash E to struggle free, or he drags you to Correction, where a friend can pull the lever to let you out.
