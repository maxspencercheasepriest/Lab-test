      How to play

      Ring the four chapel bells. Return the three film reels to the projector. Someone stands on the plate in the old classroom to raise the gate, while someone else fetches the study key. Then unlock the exit and go home.

      The Prefect hunts by sound. Walking is quiet, running is loud, crouching is silent. Talking carries. The bells carry everywhere. Hide in lockers. Throw bottles to lead him away. If he catches you, a friend can pull the lever in Correction to let you out.
